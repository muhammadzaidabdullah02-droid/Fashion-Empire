International Importer FAQ

Frequently Asked Export Questions

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Our standard MOQ starts from just 30 to 50 pieces per style/color, making it easy for emerging fashion labels and athletic academies to test market demand with minimal risk.

How fast can you deliver pre-production samples?

Custom samples with your private labels, sublimation prints, or embroidery are completed within 5 to 7 business days and dispatched via DHL Express worldwide.

What international payment methods are accepted?

We accept Bank Wire Transfer (T/T), Letter of Credit (L/C) for volume contracts, Western Union, and MoneyGram. Production starts with a 50% deposit and 50% balance before shipping.

How are international customs and freight managed?

We offer DDP (Delivered Duty Paid), FOB, and CIF terms. We prepare all commercial invoices, packing lists, and Certificates of Origin for smooth customs clearance.
